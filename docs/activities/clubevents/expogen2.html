What is EXPOGEN 2.0?
EXPOGEN 2.0 is a student-driven Project Expo organized by the SCET – NEXUS Coding Club. It provides a platform for showcasing innovation, technical skills, and practical problem-solving across software, AI, web development, and emerging technologies.
Who can participate or attend?
Anyone! The event is open to all SCET students across all departments and year groups. Tech enthusiasts, alumni, and faculty are also welcome. No prerequisites needed.
Is there a registration fee?
No! EXPOGEN 2.0 is completely free. Just register, get your entry pass, and show up on April 6th at the SCET Auditorium.
What kinds of projects can I showcase?
Any technical project — software, AI/ML, web apps, IoT, mobile apps, hardware, data science — across any domain. If you built it, you can showcase it.
Can I attend without a project?
Absolutely! Attend as an audience member, explore projects, network, and get inspired. No requirement to present in order to attend.
How are projects judged?
Projects are evaluated by faculty judges on innovation, technical complexity, practical application, and presentation quality. Winners are announced at 3:30 PM closing ceremony.
