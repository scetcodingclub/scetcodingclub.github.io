Learner's Path

NEXUS Learners Circle
If you're someone who wants to grow every single day — this is for you. In the Learner's Path, you'll focus on improving your coding, exploring new topics, and sharing your learning journey with others. You'll get to join regular challenges, learn through community discussions, and even take short sessions to explain what you've learned. It's not about being perfect — it's about being consistent, curious, and passionate about learning something new each day. Perfection isn't the goal — express yourself freely.

Contributor's Path

NEXUS Core Contributors
This is for those who love building, creating, and making things happen. Being a Contributor isn't just about technical work — it's about taking initiative, coming up with ideas, and helping the club grow. You might work on events, designs, content, or community projects, depending on your interest. Along the way, you'll improve your leadership, teamwork, and creative thinking. You'll learn how to turn ideas into reality with the people around you — and that's what makes it exciting.

All-Rounder Path

Both Learner & Contributor
If you want to explore both sides — learning and contributing — this path is for you. You'll be part of the daily learning circle while also helping in club activities, projects, or events. It's a balanced way to grow your technical skills while building real-world experience through teamwork and collaboration. You'll learn how to manage your time, share what you know, and make an impact beyond just coding.
