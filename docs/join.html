How You'll Join the Journey

Here's the flow that takes you into the NEXUS community.

1

Choose Your Path

Pick where you fit best Learner, Contributor or All Rounder. Once you choose, submit your application and explore your path.

2

Interaction Session

If your application is approved, you'll be invited for an interaction session. This session helps us know more about you your goals, interests and ideas so we can guide you better with in NEXUS

3

Get Welcomed

If your interaction session is approved, you'll officially become part of NEXUS. You'll be added to our community Groups and get access to upcoming onboarding sessions and events.

4

Begin Your Journey

Start exploring projects, group discussions, and community events. Learn, collaborate, and grow with the NEXUS.

Learner's Path

NEXUS Learners Circle
If you're eager to grow a little every day, this is for you. Here, you'll improve your coding, explore new ideas and share what you learn with others through small sessions and discussions. It's all about being consistent, curious and passionate not perfect.

Contributor's Path

NEXUS Core Contributors
For those who love to build, create and take initiative. You'll help organise events, design content, or work on club projects turning ideas into reality while learning teamwork and leadership along the way.

All-Rounder Path

Both Learner & Contributor
For those who want the best of both worlds, learning and contributing. You'll grow your skills through coding and collaboration while helping in projects and events that make a real impact.
